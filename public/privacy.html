Taily privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Taily keeps what you enter on your own device. There is no account, no Taily server, no advertising and no analytics. The developer never receives your expenses, notes, budgets or anything else you put into the app.

What Taily stores, and where

Everything you add lives in a database on your device: in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's storage on the web version. That includes:

  • expenses and income, with amounts, dates, tags, notes and the account they belong to
  • accounts, budgets, savings goals, recurring payments, and lent and borrowed entries
  • your settings, and your app-lock PIN if you set one (kept as a one-way hash, never as plain text)

Taily does not send any of this anywhere.

Permissions on Android, and why

  • Microphone, only when you hold the + button to add an expense by voice. Your phone's speech recognition turns what you say into text and Taily keeps only that text. Taily never records or stores audio. Depending on your phone, the speech service (provided by Google or your phone's maker) may process audio on the device or on its servers, under its own privacy policy.
  • Notifications, to remind you about lent or borrowed money and to flag budget alerts.
  • Vibration, for the small haptic tap when you press things.
  • Internet, for the requests listed below.

Taily reads a file only when you pick one to import (a bank statement as CSV or PDF) or share one into Taily, and writes one only when you export your data as a CSV. A statement shared into Taily goes straight into the import preview, and Taily deletes its own copy of the file as soon as it has read it.

Network requests

When you switch your currency, Taily asks open.er-api.com for the current exchange rate. The request carries the currency code and nothing about you, but like any website that service can see your IP address.

The APK distributed outside Google Play also checks this project's public releases page on GitHub to see whether a newer version exists. The Google Play version does not.

The web version is served from jatin-dhir.github.io/snowy, hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits to that site.

Backups

If automatic backup is on in your phone's settings, Android includes Taily's data in your Google backup only when that backup is end-to-end encrypted, which needs a screen lock on the phone. Otherwise Taily's data is left out, and on Android 8 and older it is never included. Backups are handled by your phone and Google, not by Taily. On the web version your data lives in your browser, so clearing the site's data deletes it. Settings, then Export as CSV, gives you a copy you control.

An encrypted backup (part of Taily Pro) is a file you save or send wherever you choose. It is locked with a password that Taily never stores, so without that password nobody can read it, Taily included.

Purchases

Taily Pro is bought through Google Play, which handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Taily never sees your payment details. It only asks Google Play whether Pro is unlocked on your account and keeps that answer on your phone.

Sharing and selling

Taily does not sell or share your data. It has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete entries, or a whole profile, inside the app. Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing the site data for the web version, erases everything Taily stored. The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Taily is a personal finance tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.

Contact
