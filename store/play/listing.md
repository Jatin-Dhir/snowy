## Full description (4000 max)

Taily is an expense tracker built to stay out of your way. Open it, type the amount, tap a tag. Logged in about three seconds.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account to make, no cloud to sync, and no ads.

Log in a moment
• Amount-first number pad with one-tap tag suggestions that learn from your own habits
• Hold the + button and just say it: "250 shopping" or "got 2000 from Rahul"
• Spent and received, with tags you can rename, recolour and add to
• Notes are remembered: tag "Zepto" as Groceries once and Taily files it there next time

Know where it went
• Safe to spend today: your monthly budget turned into one number you can trust
• Budgets for the month and for each tag, with a pace line that shows whether you are ahead or behind
• Insights with a clear breakdown, a six-month view and a monthly report with plain-language tips
• Savings goals you can add to and take out of

Keep track of people and payments
• Lent and borrowed: who owes whom, with reminders and a settled history
• Recurring payments log themselves on their day
• Several accounts (UPI, cash, card, or your own), each with its own balance

Bring your history in
• Import a bank statement from a CSV or PDF, or share one straight into Taily from your files, email or bank app
• Export everything to CSV whenever you like

Made for India, works anywhere
• Rupees by default, with live conversion if you switch currency
• Light and dark themes with soft pastel accent colours
• Optional app lock with a 4-digit PIN

Private by design
Taily keeps your entries on your device and never uploads them. Read the full policy at taily.jatindhir.com/privacy.html
