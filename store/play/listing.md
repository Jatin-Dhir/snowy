Taily: Expense Tracker

## Short description (80 max)

## Full description (4000 max)

Taily is an expense tracker built to stay out of your way. Open it, type the amount, tap a tag. Logged in about three seconds.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account to make, no cloud to sync, and no ads.

Log in a moment
• Amount-first number pad with one-tap tag suggestions that learn from your own habits, and crisp key clicks as you type
• Hold the + and just say it: "250 shopping", "dhai sau ka khana kal" or "got 2000 from Rahul". You see what Taily understood before it saves, with Undo right there
• Voice from anywhere: a home-screen widget, a Quick Settings tile and a launcher shortcut start listening the moment you tap them
• Spent and received, with tags you can rename, recolour and add to
• Notes are remembered: tag "Zepto" as Groceries once and Taily files it there next time

Trips with friends
• Start a trip and add friends by name. Every spend you add splits between you by itself
• Someone else paid, or someone skipped dinner? One tap. Or just say "Rahul paid 800 for the cab"
• At the end, who pays whom in the fewest payments. Only your share counts as your spending
• Send the group a link to see every spend. Nobody needs an account, or even the app

Know where it went
• Safe to spend today: your monthly budget turned into one number you can trust
• Budgets for the month and for each tag, with a pace line that shows whether you are ahead or behind
• Insights with a clear breakdown, a six-month view and a monthly report with plain-language tips
• Savings goals you can add to and take out of

Keep track of people and payments
• Lent and borrowed: who owes whom, with reminders and a settled history
• Several accounts (UPI, cash, card, or your own), each with its own balance

Made for India, works anywhere
• Rupees by default, with live conversion if you switch currency
• Light and dark themes with soft pastel accent colours
• App lock with a 4-digit PIN that locks again when you step away
• Export everything to CSV whenever you like

Taily Pro: pay once, keep it for good
• Import bank statements from CSV, Excel or PDF, or share one straight in from your files, email or bank app
• Encrypted backups: your whole ledger in one password-locked file, ready for a new phone
• Recurring payments that log themselves on their day
• Profiles: separate books for family or a shop
• Any accent colour you like
No subscription. Everything else in Taily is free.

Private by design
Taily keeps your entries on your device and never uploads them. Read the full policy at jatin-dhir.github.io/snowy/privacy.html
