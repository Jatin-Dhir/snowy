Voice understands what you say. Amounts said in pieces ("two fifty") come out right, and so do Hindi numbers like pachpan, Devanagari, and dates like "on the 3rd". "Groceries 800 from DMart" is a spend, "Mom sent 5000" is money in. G Pay, Phone Pay and "card se" set how you paid, and on a trip "Mira paid 1500" finds Meera.
